Add to Info.plist (or Target > Info > "Fonts provided by application"):

<key>UIAppFonts</key>
<array>
    <string>BarlowCondensed-SemiBold.ttf</string>
    <string>BarlowCondensed-Bold.ttf</string>
    <string>SourceSans3-Medium.ttf</string>
    <string>SourceSans3-SemiBold.ttf</string>
    <string>SourceSans3-Bold.ttf</string>
</array>

Usage (PostScript names):
Font.custom("BarlowCondensed-Bold", size: 28)
Font.custom("SourceSans3-Medium", size: 16)
